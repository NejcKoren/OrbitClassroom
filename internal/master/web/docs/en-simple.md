# Orbit Classroom — the easy guide

Hello! This short guide explains Orbit Classroom in plain words. You do **not** need to be good with computers. Just read one step at a time. If you want the long, detailed version, click **Full guide** at the top left.

## What is this?

Orbit Classroom lets a **teacher** see the screens of the **students'** computers and help the whole class from one place — a bit like looking over everyone's shoulder, but from the teacher's desk. You can show your screen to everyone, send a message, lock the screens to get attention, and more.

There are two programs:

- **Orbit Master** — this one. It runs on the **teacher's** computer. You are looking at it right now.
- **Orbit Agent** — a small helper that goes on each **student's** computer. You install it once and then forget about it.

> **Word helper:** *install* means "copy the program onto the computer and switch it on, once." *Network* means the computers that are joined together at your school (usually the same room or building).

## Set it up in 3 steps

### Step 1 — Get the student program

Click the big **Download setup package** button (you'll see it on the welcome screen, or go to **Find computers** on the left). Your computer saves a file called **Orbit-Agent-Setup.zip**. A *zip* is just a box with files inside.

### Step 2 — Put it on each student computer

Copy that file onto each student's computer. You can use a **USB stick** (a memory stick you plug in), or a shared network folder if your school has one.

On the student computer:

1. **Double-click** the zip file to open the box.
2. **Double-click** the file named **Install-Orbit-Agent**.
3. A blue or yellow Windows window may pop up asking *"Do you want to allow this app to make changes?"* — click **Yes**. (This is normal. Windows always asks before installing anything.)
4. Done! That computer is ready. You can move to the next one.

> If Windows says **"Windows protected your PC"**, click the small grey words **More info**, then click **Run anyway**. This only happens because the program is new, not because it is unsafe.

### Step 3 — Find the computers

Back on the teacher computer, click **Find computers** on the left, then **Search network**. The student computers will appear by themselves. Tick the ones you want and click **Add selected**. That's it — you're finished! 🎉

## Using it in class

Click a computer picture to **choose** it. Click the buttons at the top to do things. Here is what each button means, in one line:

- **Demo** — show your screen to the class.
- **Lock** — freeze the students' screens so they look at you.
- **View** — watch one student's screen up close.
- **Control** — use one student's mouse and keyboard yourself, to help them.
- **Message** — send a short note that pops up on their screen.
- **Poll** — ask a quick question; everyone taps an answer.
- **Website** — open a web page on their computers.
- **Run** — start a program on their computers.
- **Send file** — copy a file onto their desktops.
- **Screenshot** — save a picture of their screens.
- **Focus** — close games and chat apps during the lesson.
- **Power** — turn computers on, restart them, or shut them down.

To **undo** locking, click the **Lock** button again — it now says **Unlock**.

Tip: if you don't choose any computer first, the button works for **all** of them at once.

## What do students see?

Orbit is honest — it never hides. Each student sees a little **Orbit circle** near their clock. When you are watching, a **red dot** appears and the student gets a small message that says their teacher is looking. Students can also **raise a hand** by clicking that circle, and you will see it.

## Is my teacher key safe?

Yes. Think of it like a house key. Only your Orbit Master can open (control) the student computers. The part that goes onto student computers is like the lock — it can't open anything by itself. Keep the teacher computer signed in with a password and you're fine.

## If something doesn't work

- **A computer says "Offline" (grey).** It is turned off, or nobody has signed in yet, or it isn't on the same network. Turn it on and sign in.
- **Nothing is found when I search.** Make sure the little Orbit circle is showing near the clock on the student computer. Try the **Deep scan** tickbox. If you know the computer's number (like `PC-12`), type it in by hand under **Find computers**.
- **The picture is black.** Windows is showing its own safety screen on that computer (for example a password box). This is normal; it clears by itself.
- **It won't let the student close it.** That's on purpose, if your school installed the protected version — only a teacher or an IT person with an admin password can remove it. See **Full guide → "Remove"** if you need to uninstall.

## Need a hand?

Ask the person who looks after the computers at your school (often called "IT"). Show them the **Full guide** — it has all the technical details they might want. Everything you do in Orbit can be undone, so feel free to click around and try things.

You've got this. Enjoy your calmer classroom! 😊
