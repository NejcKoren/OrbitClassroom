LANGUAGE PACKS / JEZIKOVNI PAKETI
=================================

EN  Put additional languages into this folder (next to OrbitMaster.exe):
      <code>.json   texts of the teacher program, e.g. de.json
                    (copy en.json from the source code, translate the values,
                     set "_name" to the language name)
      <code>.md     the guide shown under "Help & guide" (optional)
    Restart Orbit Master afterwards. Missing texts are shown in English.
    Student texts: agent-<code>.json next to OrbitAgent.exe (see GUIDE_EN.txt, 14.1).

SL  Dodatne jezike shranite v to mapo (ob datoteki OrbitMaster.exe):
      <koda>.json   besedila učiteljevega programa, npr. de.json
                    (kopirajte en.json iz izvorne kode, prevedite vrednosti,
                     "_name" nastavite na ime jezika)
      <koda>.md     navodila v razdelku »Pomoč in navodila« (neobvezno)
    Nato znova zaženite Orbit Master. Manjkajoča besedila so prikazana v angleščini.
    Besedila za učence: agent-<koda>.json ob OrbitAgent.exe (glejte NAVODILA_SL.txt, 14.1).
